# Standard Reorder Quantity Guidelines

## 1. Primary Rule
The recommended reorder quantity should normally be the **static Reorder Quantity** stored in the inventory record.

## 2. Use of Average Monthly Usage
Average Monthly Usage may be used only to explain why a particular quantity is recommended.  
It must not override the static Reorder Quantity unless the procurement officer explicitly changes it.

## 3. Adjustment Principles
- Do not recommend a quantity lower than the static Reorder Quantity without human approval.
- Very large increases above the static quantity require owner approval.
- Recommended quantity must always be a positive integer.
