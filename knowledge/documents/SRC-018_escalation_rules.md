# Escalation Rules for Critical Stock-Outs

A low-stock situation should be treated as **urgent / critical** when any of the following is true:

1. Current stock is zero or negative.
2. Current stock is less than 30% of the reorder point and the item has high average monthly usage.
3. The item is classified as a critical operational material (e.g., key raw materials or fast-moving construction items).

In such cases the system should highlight the item as high priority and recommend immediate human review.
