# Seasonal Demand Notes – Construction Materials

## Dry Season (approximately December – February and June – August)
- Higher demand for cement, iron sheets, and timber as construction activity increases.
- Lead times may become longer due to higher order volumes.

## Rainy Season (approximately March – May and September – November)
- Demand for outdoor construction materials often slows down.
- Some projects pause, leading to lower consumption of cement and iron sheets.
- Good period for reviewing stock levels and negotiating prices.

These are general observations only and should not be treated as exact forecasts.
