# Lead Time Reference Table

Typical lead times for common items used by SMEs in Uganda (construction and hardware sector):

| Item Category              | Typical Lead Time | Notes                              |
|---------------------------|-------------------|------------------------------------|
| Cement                    | 3–7 days          | Local distributors usually faster  |
| Iron Sheets               | 5–10 days         | Depends on gauge and quantity      |
| Timber / Wood             | 4–8 days          | Seasoned timber may take longer    |
| Nails, Screws, Fasteners  | 2–5 days          | Widely available                   |
| Paint & Solvents          | 3–6 days          |                                    |
| Plumbing fittings         | 5–12 days         | Imported items take longer         |
| Electrical materials      | 4–10 days         |                                    |

These are indicative figures only. Always check the actual lead time stated on the supplier quotation.
