# Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) Notes

Many suppliers apply Minimum Order Quantities. Typical examples:

- Cement: Often sold in full truck loads or minimum 20–50 bags
- Iron Sheets: Frequently sold in bundles (e.g., 20 or 50 pieces)
- Fasteners and small hardware: May have packet or carton MOQs

When the recommended reorder quantity is below a known MOQ, the procurement officer should be informed so they can decide whether to increase the order or find an alternative supplier.
