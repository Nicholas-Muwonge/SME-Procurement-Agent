# Unit Conversion Guide – Hardware & Building Materials

This guide provides common unit conversions used in SME procurement for construction and hardware items in Uganda.

## Cement
- 1 bag of cement = 50 kg
- Always record cement in “bags” unless otherwise specified

## Iron Sheets
- Iron sheets are normally counted in **pieces**
- Do not convert to square metres unless the exact dimensions are known and required

## Timber
- Commonly measured in **pieces** or **metres**
- When length is important, record both quantity and length ( 10 pieces × 4 metres)

## Nails, Screws and Fasteners
- Usually measured in **kilograms** or **packets**
- Small quantities may be recorded in pieces when necessary

## Paint
- Measured in **litres** (1 litre, 4 litres, 20 litres containers are common)

## General Rule
Always keep the original unit of measure that appears in the inventory record and supplier quotation.  
Only perform a conversion when it is clearly required for comparison or calculation, and show both the original and converted values.
