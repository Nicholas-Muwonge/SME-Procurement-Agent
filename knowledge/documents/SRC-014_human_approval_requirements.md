# Human Approval Requirements

## Core Rule
No purchase requisition may leave “Draft” status and proceed to ordering, supplier selection, or payment without explicit human approval.

## System Behaviour
- The system may generate a draft requisition.
- The system may recommend quantities and show quotations.
- The system is strictly forbidden from:
  - Selecting a final supplier
  - Placing an order
  - Committing funds
  - Sending documents to suppliers

All final decisions remain with the procurement officer or the business owner.
