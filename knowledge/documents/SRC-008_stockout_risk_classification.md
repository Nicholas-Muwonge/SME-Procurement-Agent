# Stock-Out Risk Classification

When an item falls below its reorder point, classify the risk as follows:

- **High Risk**: Current stock is less than 50% of the reorder point, or the item is a fast-moving critical material.
- **Medium Risk**: Current stock is between 50% and 100% of the reorder point.
- **Low Risk**: Current stock is exactly at or only slightly below the reorder point and usage is slow.

This classification helps the procurement officer prioritise which items to handle first.
