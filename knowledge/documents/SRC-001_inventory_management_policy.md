# SME Inventory Management Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines how inventory levels are monitored and when items should be flagged for reordering in order to avoid stock-outs while controlling excess stock.

## 2. Reorder Rule
An item must be flagged as “Reorder Required” when:
**Current Stock ≤ Reorder Point**

## 3. Responsibilities
- The system is responsible for detecting and flagging items that meet the reorder rule.
- The procurement officer is responsible for reviewing the recommendation and deciding the final action.
- No automatic purchasing is allowed.

## 4. Review Frequency
Inventory levels should be reviewed at least once per day or whenever a significant stock movement occurs.

## 5. Safety Stock
Reorder points already include a small safety stock buffer. Do not add extra quantity unless specially approved by the owner.
