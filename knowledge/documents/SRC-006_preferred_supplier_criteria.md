# Preferred Supplier Criteria

When comparing quotations, the following factors should be considered (in order of importance):

1. **Unit Price** – Lowest total cost for the required quantity.
2. **Lead Time** – Ability to deliver within the needed timeframe.
3. **Quotation Validity** – Only valid (non-expired) quotations should be considered.
4. **Reliability** – Past performance and consistency of the supplier.
5. **Payment Terms** – Favourable terms are preferred but not mandatory.

The system may rank or highlight suppliers based on price and lead time, but the final selection of a supplier must always be made by a human.
